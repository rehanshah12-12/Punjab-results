Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I check my result?

Go to the Results page, select your board and open the official board result website.

Do you store my roll number?

This website does not require you to create an account or submit your roll number to us.

Can I get result updates?

Yes. You can visit our Updates page and join our WhatsApp channel for announcements.

Is Punjab Results an official board website?

No. Punjab Results is an independent informational website. Official results are provided by the respective education boards.
